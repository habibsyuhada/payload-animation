## Virus archetypes

- **Brute Rush**: Satu baris: Brute Force I + Exploit I + jalan ke Core (2190 KB) — agresi murni, tanpa evasi atau sustain.
- **Ghost Crawler**: Memutari Honeypot yang terdeteksi, lalu merayap sambil memperbaiki diri (2280 KB) — nol blok serang.
- **Scanner Hunter**: Exploit hanya saat berdiri di Firewall, Brute Force selalu (2350 KB) — kombo contoh GDD §4.2, sekarang bisa ditulis apa adanya.
- **Survivor**: Self Repair II + Decoy I yang menyala di bawah 50% Integrity, agresi konstan di bawahnya (2380 KB).
- **Ghost Scout**: Sensor maksimal, tempur nol (2370 KB): selalu memutari bahaya, dan merayap begitu ada Honeypot/Trap dalam radius.
- **Swarm Bomber**: Selalu: pecah jadi 2 tubuh + jalan ke Core; begitu salah satu tubuh menduduki Breach Node, ia meledakkan diri (2970 KB, account tier 3) — worm-split + detonate, GDD §4.2's deferred combo.
- **Ghost Memory**: Menyalakan penanda begitu Honeypot terdeteksi, lalu SELAMANYA jalan acak alih-alih menuju Core sampai battle berakhir (2360 KB) — set-flag/flag-is, PLAN.md B.3's "sheet yang punya ingatan".
- **Support Hunter**: Satu baris: jalan ke Core + Serang Node Pendukung I + Brute Force I (2290 KB) — target-strike, RULESET §5.1's damage-yang-ditunda-sejak-v1.

## Defense archetypes

- **Firewall Wall**: Both entries converge onto ONE line: Trap I (2pt), Firewall I (3pt), ICE I close enough to cover both the firewall AND the Core beyond it (4pt) — 9pt total, every attacker takes the whole gauntlet, not just one branch of it (the pre-8.8 layout let a rush skip straight past whichever firewall it didn't happen to walk into).
- **ICE Nest**: 2x ICE II (12pt) covering a shared Firewall I + Scanner I choke point (19pt total).
- **Honeypot Maze**: Dead-end Honeypot I decoys off each entry router (lures Random Walk), real path guarded by Firewall I + Trap I (16pt total).
- **Balanced Gauntlet**: One of every non-structural node type in a single line: Scanner I, ICE I, Honeypot I decoy, Firewall I, Trap I (15pt total).
- **Patch Wall**: 2x Firewall II (10pt) healed by a Patch Server II adjacent to both (4pt) + ICE I on the entry choke (4pt) — 18pt total. Tests grind vs sustain.
- **Tarpit Maze**: Tarpit II then Tarpit I (5pt) into Firewall I + Trap I (5pt) + ICE I riding the firewall's own radius (4pt) — 14pt total. Punishes Sprint/Exploit rushes that assume full speed all the way in.
- **Jam Corridor**: Both entries converge through a Jammer II (4pt) before an ICE II (6pt) and Firewall I (3pt), with a Honeypot I decoy off to the side (3pt) — 16pt total. Sensor-reliant sheets can't see the ICE coming.
- **Turnstile Spiral**: Turnstile II (3pt) -> Honeypot I ON THE ONLY ROUTE, not a decoy (3pt) -> Firewall I (3pt) -> Turnstile I (2pt) -> Core — 11pt total. v2 Honeypot is lethal on entry regardless of detection (only an armed Decoy saves you), and there is no second path around it to detect your way past; a body that survives still can't backtrack off the firewall to try again.
- **Alarm Nest**: Alarm Relay II (5pt) sitting right before two ICE I (4pt each) and a Firewall I (3pt) — 16pt total. Echoes RULESET §9's ICE Nest, but via network-wide escalation rather than overlapping radius.
